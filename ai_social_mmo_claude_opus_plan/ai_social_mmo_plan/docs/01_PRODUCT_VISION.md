# Product Vision

## One sentence

A polished 2D realtime social world where players can have fun, decorate a life, collect status and convert a controlled part of their in-game progression into real-world AI API quota.

## Player fantasy

> “I built a funny little life with friends, became known for my style/house/events, and the time I spent in the world eventually gave me AI compute I can use for real work.”

## Design pillars

### 1. Social before competitive power
No combat power ladder in the MVP. Progression is identity, collection, housing, reputation and creator status.

### 2. Humor is systemic
Funny item names, strange NPCs, absurd event rules, expressive emotes and player-created situations generate humor. Avoid relying on scripted jokes alone.

### 3. Multiple valid ways to grind
Players can rotate between fishing, deliveries, short jobs, events, trading, housing and social activities. No single activity should dominate the economy forever.

### 4. AI is a real-world utility reward
AI quota is a controlled reward pool, not an infinite faucet. The player can take the credential outside the game.

### 5. High polish, low friction
One-click web entry. Desktop packaging reuses the same client. Consistent UI and clear states everywhere.

## Core retention loop

```text
Enter town
  -> choose an activity
  -> earn Coin/Fame
  -> buy/style/build/participate
  -> unlock more activities and AI reward eligibility
  -> save toward an AI Credit redemption
  -> receive real API quota
  -> return for events, collection and social status
```

## Why the references matter

- Habbo demonstrates the strength of rooms, furniture, avatar identity and player-created social games.
- Pixadom demonstrates the combination of browser-based 2D social play, apartments, events and leaderboards.
- Everskies demonstrates fashion-first identity, creator submissions and a dense social/feed interface.
- Highrise demonstrates event-centric social loops, fashion challenges, custom spaces, creator ecosystems and reward-forward event UI.

The implementation should **combine patterns without cloning the visual language or assets**.
